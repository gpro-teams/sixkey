# Sixkey name and logo policy

"Sixkey" and the Sixkey icon are trademarks of VIEW TECH LTD, used without registration: no trademark has been
registered for them. Sixkey is a product of the g.pro family, published and maintained by VIEW TECH LTD.

The code of Sixkey is open source: you can read it, build it, change it and share your changes under its licences
([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)). The **name** "Sixkey" and the **Sixkey icon** are different. They tell users that a copy of
the extension, or a sync service, comes from this project, so that people can tell the real one from a look-alike. An
authenticator is an obvious target for impersonation, and this policy exists to protect its users. The licences do not
grant any right to the name or the icon.

## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your software or service **works with Sixkey**, **is compatible with Sixkey's sync protocol**, or
  **is based on Sixkey**, in plain text, without suggesting that the project endorses it.
- Use the name to refer to this project in articles, reviews, talks, tutorials and research.
- Build and use your own unmodified copy of the extension, for yourself or inside your organisation, under its name.

## What needs a different name

- **Forks and modified builds** that you distribute must use a different name and a different icon, both in the
  extension and in any store listing. The listing name must not contain "Sixkey". You may say "based on Sixkey".
- **Hosted services** running the Sixkey server, or any compatible server, must not call themselves "Sixkey",
  "Sixkey Cloud", "Sixkey Sync" or anything that suggests they are the project's own service.
- **Domains, accounts and app names** that could be mistaken for the project's own must not use the name.

## The icon

The Sixkey icon is the project's own artwork (generated by `extension/scripts/gen-icons.mjs`). Use it only to refer to
this project, unchanged, and not as the icon of a fork or another product.

## If ownership changes

If the name, the extension, its store listing, its signing keys or the hosted service are ever transferred to another
person or company, the transfer will be announced publicly in advance ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## Other marks

"Chrome", "YubiKey", "Google Authenticator" and other names that appear in this repository belong to their owners and
are used only to describe compatibility.
